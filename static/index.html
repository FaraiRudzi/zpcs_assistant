How to read this. Precision is the share of retrieved passages that were the right one. Recall is the share of test questions where the right passage was found. F1 balances the two. Test questions are built from the headings of sections the model never trained on. Precision is naturally low because the system returns up to 5 passages while only 1 or 2 are relevant, so Top-1 accuracy, recall and MRR are the better indicators. Scores show improvement on your own documents and are not a guarantee for every real question.
